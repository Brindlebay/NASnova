Privacy Policy

NASnova — last updated November 5, 2026

In short

NASnova plays your movies directly from your NAS, on your network. It asks for no account and contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The publisher has no server and receives no data about you.

Your NAS

The NAS address, share name, user name and chosen folders are stored in the app; the password is stored in the device Keychain, never in plain text. The app reads your folders and files without ever changing, moving or deleting them. These exchanges take place between the device and your NAS, without going through the publisher.

Posters and summaries

To show posters, summaries and details, the app sends only the title and year of a movie or series (or its ID with these services) to public metadata services: TMDB, TVmaze, Wikipedia and Wikidata, and TheTVDB, OMDb or Fanart.tv if you enter a key. Never your file names, your folders, your NAS address or your credentials. The keys you enter stay in the device Keychain. You can also use none of these services: the images and .nfo files in your folders, and the posters you import, stay on the device. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Demo movies

If you choose “Try the demo”, ten free movies are streamed over the internet from Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org), as if from a NAS. Their details and posters are built into the app: nothing is asked of a metadata service.

Data stored on the device

Your library index, posters, resume points, watched movies, My List, fixed details, settings and downloaded movies (iPhone and iPad) are stored only on the device and deleted with the app. Cached posters older than 6 months are erased.

iCloud

If iCloud sync is on (in the app's settings), your NAS and their folders, watched movies, My List, resume points and fixed details are also stored in your iCloud, so you find them on your other devices. Passwords only go through iCloud Keychain, end-to-end encrypted; the Apple TV asks for them once. This data stays in your iCloud account: the publisher has no access to it.

What the services see

As with any internet connection, the services mentioned see the public IP address of your router. Their own privacy policies apply.

Your rights

As the app sends no personal data to the publisher, the publisher holds none to access, correct or delete. To erase what is stored, delete the app; for iCloud, turn sync off in the app or use iCloud storage management in Settings. Any question: brindlebay.support@gmail.com.
