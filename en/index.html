Setting up my NAS

On first launch, the setup assistant finds your NAS on the network, then guides you step by step: protocol, account, folders, posters and import. It can be run again from Settings, “Setup Assistant”. The iPhone, iPad or Apple TV must be on the same network as the NAS.

Which NAS?

Any NAS that shares its folders over SMB (SMB2 and SMB3, and SMB1 for older models such as the D-Link DNS-320), NFS or WebDAV, for example a Synology, QNAP, Western Digital or D-Link, or a computer sharing a folder. The app never changes your files.

“The NAS is not responding”

A sleeping NAS takes a few seconds to wake up: the app retries by itself. Otherwise, check that it is on and that the device is on the same network (not the guest network of your router). With the assistant, the app also explains a refusal: password, account permissions on the share, protocol turned off.

Formats and playback

HEVC and H.264, in HDR10, HLG and Dolby Vision when the screen allows, are played by the app's native player, with every audio track (Dolby Digital, DTS, TrueHD) and SRT, ASS and PGS subtitles. Other formats are played by VLC. An automatic playback buffer absorbs network hiccups. When you leave the app during a movie on iPhone or iPad, the sound keeps playing and the movie stays in the Dynamic Island; a paused movie waits for you.

Posters and summaries

TVmaze and Wikipedia work with nothing to set up. For the best details, create a free key at themoviedb.org and enter it in Settings. A wrong match is fixed with “Fix details”; you can also import your own poster.

Apple TV and iCloud

The NAS you add on the iPhone reach the Apple TV through iCloud. For your security, passwords are never sent to the Apple TV: enter them once on it. On the Apple TV, “Refresh” at the top of each tab looks for new movies at once.

Watching without the NAS

On iPhone and iPad, “Download” copies a movie to the device to watch it offline. Copies are managed in Settings, “Downloads”.

Trying without a NAS

“Try the demo” (on first launch, or at step 1 of the assistant) adds ten free movies streamed over the internet. They are removed in Settings, “My NAS”.

Languages

NASnova is in English and French. It follows your device's language; on iPhone and iPad you can also choose it in Settings › NASnova › Language.
